Quotes & pricing

The quote is free, and once given it is fixed. You describe the fault, I diagnose the device, and you get one price covering parts and labour before any work begins. Nothing is ordered and nothing is opened up further without your go-ahead, so there is never a surprise line at the end.

Yes, for most jobs. Send me the make, the model and what the device is doing, ideally with a photo or a short video, and I will give you a realistic range straight away. The firm price comes after the diagnostic, because guessing at a fault nobody has tested yet is how people end up paying twice.

How a repair works

First the symptoms, in your words. Then the diagnostic: power delivery, memory, storage health, thermals and software, depending on the fault. Then I call or message you with what failed, the options and a fixed price. Once you approve it I carry out the repair, test it under load, and hand it back with a note of exactly what was done and which part went in.

Most diagnostics are done the same day. Phone batteries are often an hour, laptop batteries and in-stock screens the same day, and common repairs such as drives, memory, cooling or a software cleanup typically 24 to 48 hours. Sourced parts or board-level work take longer, and you get an estimated date with the quote rather than after.

Data, parts & warranty

Your data is yours. I only access what the repair requires, I never copy your files elsewhere, and I never share anything with anyone. Always back up before handing over a device, and if the job carries any risk, such as a failing drive, a board repair or a reinstall, I image the disk first so your files survive even if the hardware does not.

I fit original (OEM) parts whenever they are available, because they perform and last best, and tested premium-grade equivalents when they are not. Either way you know which part is going into your device before I fit it. My workmanship is guaranteed and parts carry their manufacturer or supplier warranty, with a written note of what was done.
